Data Retention & Deletion

Account & Data Deletion

Compliance with Google Play & App Store Standards

1. Overview & Immediate In-App Deletion

In accordance with user privacy rights and app store data safety regulations, Outloop provides an instantaneous and permanent self-serve mechanism to delete your account and all associated data.

To delete your account immediately inside the app:

  1. Navigate to the Profile tab in Outloop.
  2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the warning prompt.

2. What Data Is Permanently Purged

Upon triggering account deletion, the following data is irrevocably deleted:

  • Authentication Records: Your Firebase Authentication user account, user UID, linked Google token, email address, display name, and avatar URL are deleted immediately from Firebase servers.
  • Local Application State: Your local preferences, enabled networks, custom filter overrides, strict mode configurations, and notification permissions are wiped.
  • Usage Analytics & Counters: All local statistics (number of short videos hidden, feeds blocked, and time saved) are purged from your device storage.

3. Data Retention Timeline

Deletion is immediate for user identity and local data. Any operational server logs related to authentication events are automatically expunged within 30 days as per Google Cloud standard security protocols.

4. Manual Deletion Requests

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to access your device, you can request a manual deletion of your account records by emailing our data support team from the email address associated with your Outloop account.
