Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

Intent was created with a single core promise: we believe your attention belongs to you, not advertising algorithms. Our business model is not based on monetizing your private personal information, and our application is engineered to keep your browsing private, isolated, and local to your device.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Authentication, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture avatar strictly to maintain your account identity and sync your settings across devices.
  • Local Usage & Shielding Statistics: Counters for short videos hidden and infinite feeds blocked are computed locally on your device to display your daily focus insights. These metrics stay strictly in your device's local storage.
  • No Browsing History or Credentials: When you access social networks through Intent, you are browsing directly within a secure, sandboxed webview. Intent does not inspect, harvest, or log your account passwords, search histories, private messages, or payment details.

3. How We Use Information

Any collected information is utilized strictly to:

  • Authenticate your session securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Remember your enabled platforms and custom filter configurations.
  • Deliver non-algorithmic notification alerts for direct messages (if enabled).

4. Data Storage & Security

Authentication tokens and account profiles are safeguarded using Google Firebase's industry-standard infrastructure. Local settings and filter rules are encrypted and persisted securely in your device's protected app sandbox.

5. Third-Party Services

Intent interacts with third-party web services (e.g. YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) via standard browser sessions. Third-party sites govern their own privacy practices according to their respective policies. Intent's filter rules strip distracting feeds, algorithm recommendation carousels, and tracking elements.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated Firebase records at any time directly within the app under Profile → Delete Account.
